Let $G$ be a group. A subset $H$ is a **subgroup** of $G$ if it is a group with respect to the same binary operation. We sometimes write $H \subseteq G$ to denote that $H$ is a subgroup of $G$

**Theorem:** Let $G$ be a group and let $H \subseteq G$. Then $H$ is a subgroup if and only if
- $1_G \in H$
- $h_1 \circ h_2 \in H$ for all $h_1, h_2 \in H$ 
- $h^{-1} \in H$ for all $h \in H$
