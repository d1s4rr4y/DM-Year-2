**Definition:** Let $R$ be a ring, let $I$ be an ideal of $R$ and let $r \in R$. We call
$$r + I = \{ r + a : a \in I \}$$
a *coset* of $R$, and we let 
$$R/I = \{r+I : r \in R\}$$
be the *quotient* of $R$ by $I$.

**Theorem:** Let $I$ be an ideal of $R$. Then $R/I$ is a ring when addition and multiplication are defined by
$$(r + I) + (s+I) = (r + s) + I, \quad (r + I) \cdot (s + I) = rs + I$$ Moreover, the additive and multiplicative identity elements are respectively $0 + I = I$ and $1 + I$
