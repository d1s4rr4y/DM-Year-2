**Ring:** A *ring* is a set $R$ with operations $+, \circ$ such that $(R, +)$ is an abelian group, $R$, is associative under $\circ$, and $R$ is distributive such that $a \circ (b + c) = a \circ b + a \circ c$ for all $a, b, c \in R$. The additive identity is usually denoted $0$ and the multiplicative identity is usually denoted $1$. ^ring

**Commutative Ring:** In a *commutative ring*, multiplication is commutative.

**Ring with Unity:** A *ring with unity* has multiplicative identity $1$

**Field:** A *field* is a ring $R$ in which both operations form abelian groups.

**Left Ideal:** A *left ideal* is a subring $A \subset R$ with $ar \in A$ for all $a \in A, r \in R$. A right ideal is similarly defined. 

**Ideal:** An *ideal* is a subring $A \subset R$ which is both a left and a right ideal. 

**Quotient Ring:** Given an ideal $A \subset R$, a *quotient ring* is the set of (additive) cosets of $A$, denoted $R/A$.
