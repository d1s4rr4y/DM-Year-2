# Special Sequences

**Power Sequence:** $\{a^n\}_{n=0}^{\infty}$ 
**Exponent Sequence:** $\{n^a\}_{n=0}^{\infty}$ 
**Binomial Sequence:** $\{\frac{n^k}{b^n}\}_{n = 0}^{\infty}$ 
**Euler's Sequence:** $\{(1 + \frac{1}{n}) ^ n\}_{n = 0}^{\infty}$ 

# Special Series
**Geometric Series:** $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a^n$ converges absolutely for $|a| < 1$ to $\frac{1}{1-a}$, diverges otherwise 
**p Series:** $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^p}$ converges for $p > 1$, diverges otherwise 
**Harmonic Series:** $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n}$ diverges
**Alternating Harmonic Series:** $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{n+1}}{n}$ converges conditionally
**Euler's Series:** $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!}$ converges to $e$
